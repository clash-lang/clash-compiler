# CHANGED
Clash now derives more names in the generated HDL from the source code:

* Arguments of data constructors are named after the record field they are passed to, e.g. `c$baudCount` instead of `c$app_arg`.
* Arguments of functions are named after the function and its parameter, e.g. `c$txStep_s` for the state of `mealy txStep`.
* Case alternatives are named after the constructor they match, e.g. `c$x_case_Idle` instead of `c$x_case_alt`.
* Pattern bindings such as `(nextState, output) = ...` are named after their variables, e.g. `nextState_output` instead of `ds`.
* Variables made up by GHC, such as `eta`, are named after the parameter of the user-defined function they're passed to. This also names ports of top entities without a `Synthesize` annotation, e.g. `request` instead of `eta`.
* User-written names are kept when they are aliases of variables Clash made up. For example, with `-fclash-split-registers`, `dSCL` in `let dSCL = fst _dI2C` names the register holding that field.
